An Online Product Catalog for Small Businesses: What to Prepare

A practical guide for small business owners · 23 November 2026

Customers may stop asking when product information is scattered across posts or difficult to find. An online catalog organizes products in one place so visitors can compare options and understand how to order. A simple website catalog may be enough; not every business needs a complex e-commerce system from day one.

Use clear and consistent photos

Use product photos that are bright enough, in focus, and representative of the actual item. Show multiple angles when important details cannot be seen in one image. Keep image sizes and backgrounds consistent so the catalog feels organized. Avoid images that could mislead customers.

Include information that helps customers decide

Provide the product name, short description, size or dimensions, materials, colors or options, package contents, usage instructions, and care information when relevant. For food products, include ingredient or allergen information when required and when accurate details are available.

Explain pricing, options, and availability

Show the price or clearly explain how to request a quote. If prices vary by size, quantity, or specification, explain those factors. Update stock and options regularly so customers do not order based on outdated information.

Make ordering straightforward

Each product should have a clear next step: order through WhatsApp, complete a form, or add the item to a cart. If orders are handled through WhatsApp, prepare a message that includes the product name or code to make the conversation easier to manage.

Organize products so they are easy to browse

Use categories customers understand rather than internal business terms. For a large catalog, add simple search or filters. Highlight selected products naturally without hiding important information about other items.

Keep information consistent across channels

Make sure product names, photos, prices, and terms on your website match your WhatsApp catalog or marketplace listings. Assign someone to maintain the information so customers do not see conflicting details.

In summary

Start with the products customers ask about most. A concise, accurate catalog that is updated regularly is more useful than a large catalog with outdated information.
