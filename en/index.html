Before You Order

I am not sure what kind of website I need. Can I consult first?

Of course. The initial consultation is free and does not require you to place an order. Tell us about your business, target customers, and website goals. We can help you consider a landing page, company profile, online catalog, or custom system.

What should I prepare before ordering?

If available, prepare your business name and profile, logo, product or service photos, price list, contact details, address, and examples of websites you like. If some items are not ready, we can discuss what is still needed during the consultation.

My small business does not have much content yet. Can you still build a website?

Yes. We can start with the essential information you already have and make a list of missing materials. Copywriting, photography, illustrations, or logo design should be discussed separately because they are not automatically included in every package.

Will the website design be tailored to my business?

The design is planned around your brand identity, business type, and website goals. You can share design references. The level of customization and included features will be explained in the proposal before work begins.

Pricing & Payment

How much does a website from NEXORA Studio cost?

The currently displayed starting price is Rp500,000 for a Landing Page. The final cost depends on page count, design, features, product quantity, integrations, and custom requirements. We will provide an itemized proposal before you approve the work.

Are domain and hosting included in the price?

Not always. Domain and hosting are discussed based on your needs and selected package. The proposal will state whether these costs are included or billed separately, who owns the accounts, the active period, and renewal costs for following years.

How does payment work? Do I have to pay everything upfront?

Payment is made in stages as agreed. Usually, a down payment is made after the scope and proposal are approved. The balance is paid after the agreed work and revisions are complete, before launch. The schedule and amounts are recorded in writing.

Will there be additional costs after the website is completed?

Recurring costs may apply to domains, hosting, third-party services, or maintenance if used. Known costs will be explained in the proposal. New features or changes outside the original scope may require an additional fee after approval.

Features & Development

Will the website work properly on mobile phones?

Yes. Websites are designed responsively to adapt to phones, tablets, and computers. The final appearance should still be reviewed together on commonly used devices and browsers before launch.

Can the website include WhatsApp buttons, catalogs, or forms?

Yes, depending on your needs and the project scope. WhatsApp buttons, contact forms, product catalogs, and other features can be planned from the start. Features requiring additional systems or custom integrations will be included in the proposal.

Can an online store accept automatic payments?

Automatic payments or payment gateway integration can be discussed for projects that need them. Availability, provider fees, account verification, and integration depend on the third-party provider and must be agreed as part of the project scope.

How long does website development take?

The timeline depends on the website type, number of pages or products, feature complexity, and how complete your materials are. We provide an estimate after understanding your requirements. Scope changes or delayed materials may affect the schedule.

After Your Website Is Complete

Can I request revisions before the website launches?

Yes. The number of revision rounds and what can be revised are stated in the proposal. Revisions help refine the agreed work, while adding new pages or features may be treated as additional work.

Will I receive website access and files when the project is complete?

The handover details—such as project files, hosting or domain access, admin accounts, and relevant documentation—will be stated in the agreement. Discuss the access you need before the project starts.

Does NEXORA provide support after the website goes live?

Post-launch support can be discussed and included in the proposal, including its duration, type, and limits. Routine maintenance, content updates, or further feature development may be separate services.

Will my website definitely appear on the first page of Google?

No one can guarantee a specific Google ranking. A website can be built with SEO foundations such as sound page structure and metadata, but search results depend on many factors, including competition, content quality, site reputation, and time.
